# Owner Input Checklist

The owner must review and provide approved values for the following checklist items before the site can transition to a public launch state.

## 1. Brand Identity & Legal Details
- [ ] **Official Company Name:** (e.g., Presura d.o.o. or Obrt Presura)
- [ ] **Owner Legal Representative Name:**
- [ ] **Company Registration details & OIB / IBAN:**
- [ ] **Owner-approved brand logo assets:** (If replacing placeholder styling)

## 2. Public Contact Information (NAP)
- [ ] **Public Phone Number:** (Replaces `PHONE_PLACEHOLDER`)
- [ ] **Public Contact Email:** (Replaces `EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER`)
- [ ] **Office/Workshop Address:** (Replaces `ADDRESS_PLACEHOLDER`)
- [ ] **Working Hours:** (Replaces `HOURS_PLACEHOLDER`)

## 3. SEO & Structured Data Activation
- [ ] **Approved Production Domain Name:** (e.g., `presura.hr`)
- [ ] **Owner authorization to enable search indexation:** (Removal of robots.txt disallow)
- [ ] **Authorization to toggle `schemaEnabled: true`** in CMS/Sanity.

## 4. Real Installation Photos & Testimonials
- [ ] **Real installation photos & descriptions:** (To replace generic demo piping/installation placeholders)
- [ ] **Signed client consent for installation photo publication:** (Toggles `publishConsentStatus: "approved"`)
- [ ] **Verified client reviews and testimonials:** (Replaces rating placeholders)

## 5. Third-Party Integrations
- [ ] **Supabase database project creation:** (Provider credentials)
- [ ] **Resend email sender domain configuration:** (Requires domain DNS modifications)
- [ ] **Cloudflare Turnstile site & secret keys:** (Domain registry keys)
